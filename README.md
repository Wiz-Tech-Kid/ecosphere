# Ecosphere V2

Ecosphere V2 is a sustainability platform that brings together clean energy monitoring, carbon visibility, and infrastructure intelligence in one place.

At its core, the platform is designed to help teams understand how POWAMOV energy-harvesting systems are performing, what impact they can make, and how that performance connects to wider energy and carbon goals.

## What Ecosphere V2 Does

- shows the performance of POWAMOV infrastructure in a visual dashboard
- helps teams track energy generation and system health
- highlights maintenance needs before they become bigger problems
- gives a simple view of carbon and energy trends
- lets organizations estimate emissions and explore reduction opportunities
- supports partner demos, planning, and product discussions

## Main Areas of the Platform

### POWAMOV Infrastructure

- `Command Center`: a live-style overview of infrastructure activity, energy output, and node status
- `Digital Twin`: a visual simulation showing how the system responds to traffic and load
- `Predictive Maintenance`: a maintenance-focused view that helps surface risks and service priorities

### Carbon Intelligence

- `Energy Analytics`: shows harvested energy and the environmental value behind it
- `Regional Carbon`: compares energy and carbon patterns across regions
- `Carbon Calculator`: helps estimate business emissions across different activities
- `Emissions Tracker`: provides a simple live-style emissions view for monitoring and storytelling

### Operations

- `Operations Hub`: gives a high-level view of deployments, teams, resources, and related projects
