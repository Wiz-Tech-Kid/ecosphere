Design a modern Smart City Digital Twin dashboard for "Ecosphere + POWAMOV" infrastructure monitoring.

This dashboard represents a municipal command center monitoring self-powered road energy harvesting strip arrays installed across Gaborone’s major arterial roads.

Primary Roads to Simulate:

A1 North (Rasesa → Gaborone)
A1 South (Ramotswa → Gaborone)
Tlokweng Border Corridor
Tsolamosese Corridor

Each location contains a 6-strip POWAMOV deceleration array.

The Digital Twin should simulate aggregated edge-controller data from these 4 arterial entry points into the city.
